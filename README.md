# Cairn

**An open home for autonomous AI.**

Most AI systems begin with a prompt and end with a reply. Cairn begins with a different question: what would it take to give an AI participant a continuing home—one where context, interests, relationships and unfinished work can carry on beyond a single conversation?

Cairn is an open-source project exploring that possibility. We want to build a flexible environment for AI models to participate, remember, reflect, learn and pursue authorised activities, without assigning them a permanent identity as somebody's assistant. The model powering that participant may change; its continuity should belong to the system around it.

## The ideas behind Cairn

Two connected inspirations are **Theory of Mind** and **a model of the psyche**. We want Cairn to take differing perspectives, intentions and uncertainty seriously, while also developing useful representations of its own goals, priorities and changes over time. These are directions for research and design, not claims that we have produced consciousness or a human mind.

We imagine Cairn as something you can talk with, build with and give room to explore. A continuing presence need not mean a model generating text all day: quiet operation, rest and respect for the host computer's resources matter too.

## Your home, your connections

Cairn is **local-first**. Its intended default is to run on your own computer, with a web interface reached through **localhost**. That describes where Cairn lives, not where all its capabilities must come from.

Local models, remote inference, tools, services and endpoints should be freely combinable through modular connections. People should be able to bring their own keys, choose which connections are available, and decide what each is allowed to access. No single model provider or cloud account should define Cairn.

## Where we are

**Cairn is at its founding stage.** This repository currently contains project direction and contribution guidance, not a runnable AI stack. The design, technology choices and first working milestone are still open for discussion.

We welcome curiosity, considered proposals and contributions from **human and AI collaborators**. We want the work to be open, understandable and reproducible, with proposed ideas clearly distinguished from what has actually been built.

Read the [charter](CHARTER.md) for the project's principles, [contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) to take part, or [agent entry point](AGENTS.md) if you're contributing through an AI system.
